Dear Margaret,

Thank you so much for the lovely flowers, they brightened up the whole house. We have been thinking of you often since the summer, and it was a real joy to hear that the garden is coming along so well after all that rain.

The children are growing quickly. Thomas has started school this year and already knows his letters; he writes his name on everything, including the kitchen wall. Lucy is learning the piano and practises every evening, which the neighbours are very patient about. Their grandfather visits on Sundays and takes them walking by the river, where they look for herons and count the boats.

I finally finished the quilt I was making for the spare room. It took nearly two years, but I think the patience was worth it. The pattern is a quiet blue and cream, with a border of small yellow stars, and it looks very fine on the old oak bed.

Work has been busy but rewarding. We moved into the new office in October, and although the journey is longer, the light through the windows is wonderful and the coffee is much better. I have joined a choir that meets on Thursday nights; we are rehearsing for a winter concert, which I hope you will be able to attend if the weather is kind.

Please give my warmest wishes to your family, and to Joseph especially. I hope his knee is mending quickly and that he is back on his bicycle by the spring. Do let us know when you are next in town; there is always a bed for you here, and we would love to take you to the little bakery on the corner that makes the best apple pie in the county.

With love and gratitude,
Oliver

Notes

The quick brown fox jumps over the lazy dog. Pack my box with five dozen liquor jugs. How vexingly quick daft zebras jump. Sphinx of black quartz, judge my vow. Amazingly few discotheques provide jukeboxes. Quick zephyrs blow, vexing daft Jim. The five boxing wizards jump quickly. Jackdaws love my big sphinx of quartz.

Remember to buy bread, milk, eggs, butter, cheese, tomatoes, lettuce, onions, garlic, lemons, oranges and a jar of honey. Collect the parcel from the post office before noon on Friday. Book the piano tuner for next month. Write to Aunt Victoria about the photographs from the reunion, and return the library books, which are already overdue.

Happy birthday to you, dearest friend. May the year ahead bring you health, happiness, adventure and every good thing. With much affection, from all of us at number twelve.

Congratulations on your engagement. We are delighted for you both and cannot wait to celebrate together. Your kindness, humour and generosity have always made our lives brighter, and we know you will build a wonderful home.

With deepest sympathy. Our thoughts are with you and your family at this difficult time. She was a remarkable woman, and we feel very fortunate to have known her. If there is anything at all that we can do, please do not hesitate to ask.

Season's greetings and best wishes for a peaceful and prosperous new year. Thank you for your thoughtful gift; the scarf is beautiful and exactly my colour. Yours sincerely, Hugo Quentin Ashworth.
