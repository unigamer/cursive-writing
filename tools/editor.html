Editing

Drag points (the brush moves neighbours too) · Alt-click or right-click a point to delete it · double-click the line to add a point · drag the red squares to move the lead-in / lead-out cuts · drag the dashed handle to change the advance · click a line to select its stroke · [ ] previous / next glyph · scroll to zoom, drag the background to pan.
